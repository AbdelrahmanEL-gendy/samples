Before your first visit

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

Usually not. You can book directly, and we’ll check what your plan needs before your visit.

What should I bring?

Your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of your medications and clothes you can move in.

How long is the first appointment?

About an hour, one on one with your therapist. Follow-up visits take 45 minutes.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking behind the building, with a step-free entrance.
