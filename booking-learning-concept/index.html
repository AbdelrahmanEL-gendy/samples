Questions before you book

Can I book and pay online?+

Yes. Choose a program and a time, then pay by card. Group bookings and gift vouchers can be added if the client offers them.

Do I get online access after a studio session?+

Yes. Your learning platform account is created when you book, and the matching course opens on the same day as your session.

Can I move my booking?+

Use the link in your confirmation email to pick a new time. The reschedule window follows the client's own policy.

Do I need two accounts?+

No. Your booking email is your login for the learning platform, and "Student login" on the website takes you there.
