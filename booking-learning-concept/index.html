Questions before you book

Still unsure? Call the studio and we'll help you pick.

Can I book and pay online?+

Yes. Pick a session, add your details and pay by card. Gift vouchers and group bookings can be added if the business offers them.

Do I get the online course after a studio session?+

Yes. Your learning platform account is created when you book, and the matching course opens the same day.

Can I move my booking?+

Use the link in your confirmation email to pick another time. The window follows the business's own policy.

Do I need two accounts?+

No. Your booking email is your login, and "Student Login" on the website takes you straight there.
